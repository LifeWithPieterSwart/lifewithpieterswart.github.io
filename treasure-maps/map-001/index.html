The Forgotten Valley

An illustrated journey into a land of high mountains, old forests, winding waters and a hidden treasure.

About this map

This is an original, fictional treasure map made for adventure and storytelling. The landmarks and treasure route are imaginative and do not represent real GPS locations.

The trail

  1. Begin at the southern forest.
  2. Follow the dotted trail towards the river crossing.
  3. Pass the forgotten stone ruins.
  4. Look for the red X in the eastern hills.

These are story clues for the illustrated map, not real-world directions.
